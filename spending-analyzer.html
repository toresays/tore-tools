About this tool. Builds a dataset from the largest awards matching your query (USAspending.gov API), then computes statistics in your browser. Obligated = committed; Outlays = paid. Correlations are Pearson over awards where both features are present. Forensic detectors are heuristics over text/metadata (e.g. redacted recipients, missing NAICS/PSC, same recipient across agencies) — they flag things to look at, not verified findings. Source: api.usaspending.gov.
