About this tool. Shows federal awards by place of performance (where the work happens), from the USAspending.gov API. Obligated = committed (transaction level, counted once per year); Outlays = paid. This is federal money flowing into a place — not a state or local government's own budget. The API limits ranges to ~FY2008+.
