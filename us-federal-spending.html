About this data. Figures are pulled live from the USAspending.gov API. Obligated totals are counted at the transaction level — each dollar counted once, in the fiscal year it was obligated, with no multi-year double-counting. Outlays (money actually paid) are reported at the award/account level and may include payments on awards that began earlier, so the paid-vs-obligated ratio is a guide, not an exact subset. Contract ceiling (base & all options) is the maximum that could still be paid. Competition, set-aside and solicitation (RFP) details come from each award’s FPDS record. NAICS applies to contracts; assistance uses CFDA. The API limits time ranges to roughly FY2008 onward; the latest fiscal year is provisional.

Sources: api.usaspending.gov/api/v2 — spending_by_award · spending_by_category · spending_over_time · awards/<id>
